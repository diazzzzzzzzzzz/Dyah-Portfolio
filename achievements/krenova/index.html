Krenova Pati 2024

Finalist — Top 9 of 78 Teams · General Category

Competition Overview

The Krenova Pati Innovation Awards 2024 was organized by the Regional Development Planning, Research, and Innovation Agency (BAPPERIDA) of Pati Regency. The competition ran from February to May 2024 and gathered innovation teams from various general categories across the region.

Our team ranked Top 9 out of 78 teams and was officially invited to present as one of the Top 20 Finalists.

Innovation Title

“Optimizing the Growth of Cosmos sulphureus as a Short-Day Plant Through LED Light”

This innovation focuses on photoperiod manipulation using Arduino-based LED automation to stimulate flowering in short-day ornamental plants.
